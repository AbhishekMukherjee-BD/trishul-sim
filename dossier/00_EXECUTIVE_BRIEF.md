- [VERIFIED-READ] **Project Identity**: Project TRISHUL-SIM is an AI-enabled Counter-Unmanned Aerial System (C-UAS) threat simulation and tactical doctrine trainer built for Smart India Hackathon (Problem ID: SIH26247) under the Ministry of Defence (MoD) / Defence Services Staff College (DSSC), Wellington.
- [INFERRED] **The Operational Problem**: Modern asymmetric warfare has weaponized low-cost drone swarms (commercial quadcopters, 150 km/h FPV kamikazes, and loitering munitions) against forward military bases. Air defense officers lack low-cost, zero-risk simulation tools to practice split-second countermeasure doctrine (soft-kill electronic warfare vs. hard-kill point defense) before live-fire drills.
- [VERIFIED-READ] **The Technical Solution**: A sovereign, 100% offline, air-gapped simulation suite integrating a 60 Hz 3D WebGL Reynolds flocking physics engine with dual real-time viewports (3D AESA Radar PPI Scope and EO/IR Thermal FLIR with GLSL infrared shaders).
- [VERIFIED-READ] **Core Architecture & Telemetry**: Built with React 19.3.0 and Three.js 0.174.0 on the client, coordinated with a zero-dependency Python 3.14 standard library MAVLink v2.0 telemetry stream (`GLOBAL_POSITION_INT`, `ATTITUDE`) running on port 8000.
- [VERIFIED-RUN] **Sovereign AI Directing Staff**: Connects locally to an on-device Ollama LLM (`mistral:latest`, 7.2B Q4_K_M) on port 11434 to evaluate operator performance across a weighted combat readiness formula (perimeter breaches, classification accuracy, EW discipline, and ammo conservation), generating authoritative tactical After-Action Reviews (AAR) without cloud egress.
- [VERIFIED-RUN] **Operational Effectors**: Operators control a 3-layer defence suite: (1) 5,000m 20° Directional RF Jammer with 2.5s dwell lock for soft-kill Return-To-Home diversion, (2) 600 m/s rocket-propelled Kinetic Net Interceptor Pod with dynamic target tracking, and (3) GNSS Area Spoofer for coordinate dispersion.
- [VERIFIED-RUN] **Current Verified Status**: 100% operational across all 3 military scenarios (Pokhran Desert Shield, Ladakh Mountain Sentinel, and Coastal Swarm Benchmark). The regression suite passes 38 out of 38 automated assertions, and the production bundle compiles cleanly with 0 errors in 3.84s.
- [INFERRED] **Primary Novelty**: Complete elimination of cloud dependencies and expensive proprietary hardware by delivering military-grade multi-agent swarm kinematics, dual-band sensor fusion, and local LLM doctrinal debriefing in an accessible browser-based trainer.
